FAQ

Questions owners ask

Do you meet my dog before the first walk?

Yes. Every new dog starts with a free meet and greet at home.

What happens if it's pouring with rain?

We still walk. Your dog gets a towel down before we drop them home.

How do you look after my keys?

We agree how keys are handled at the meet and greet, and only your walker has them.

Can I change my walk days?

Yes. Just give us a little notice and we'll move things around.
