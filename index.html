The 1948 Tote

Structured enough for a laptop, with leather handles that sit on the shoulder.

Crossbody

The Seacole Crossbody

Small, hands-free and sized for a phone, keys and a passport.

The story: named for Mary Seacole, the Jamaican-born nurse who cared for soldiers in the Crimean War.

Travel

The Tull Wash Bag

A zipped wash bag with a wipe-clean lining, made for long trips home.

The story: named for Walter Tull, who played for Tottenham Hotspur and Northampton Town and became one of the first Black officers in the British Army.

Why Kin & Carry

Bags you're proud to carry to work and across the world.

Heritage designs too often look like souvenirs. We wanted something you'd reach for on a Monday morning and still be glad of at the departure gate.

So every piece pairs a clean, grown-up shape with a story worth telling, and every print will be original.
